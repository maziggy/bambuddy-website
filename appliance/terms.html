Section 6 — Licence Key, registration, device binding

What this annex is, and is not. It describes the support included in the subscription. That support covers the Appliance: the Image, the Appliance Software, updates, registration and Licence Keys. It is not operational support for a printer fleet and it is not the Provider's service contract for running Bambuddy. That is a separate product with its own contract, its own response times and its own pricing; a Business subscription does not replace it and confers no rights under it.

Channel.

  • Both tiers: email to support@bambuddy.cool. Priority handling on the Business tier follows from the subscription, not from a separate address.
  • Telephone, chat and remote access to the Customer's system are not part of the subscription.
  • Faults in Bambuddy itself belong in the public project repository, where they are handled alike for every user, subscription or not.

Service hours. Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 CET/CEST, excluding public holidays at the Provider's seat.

Absence. The Provider may suspend the response time for up to 15 working days per calendar year in total (holiday, illness, other absence). Planned absences are announced at least seven days in advance by email and in the admin panel, unforeseen ones without undue delay. The response period does not run during an announced absence.

Response time.

  • Personal: best efforts, no undertaking.
  • Business: normally on the next working day, and within two working days at the latest, from receipt of the request within service hours.
  • What is promised is a response, not a fix. A time to fix cannot be promised (Section 4(4)).

Covered.

  • installation and first setup of the Image on hardware from the published hardware list
  • faults in the Appliance Software itself
  • updates to the Appliance Software and rolling them back
  • registration, Licence Keys, device replacement under Section 6(3)

Not covered.

  • migrating data from an existing Bambuddy installation (see "Chargeable additional services")
  • restoring the Customer's data, in particular after failure or corruption of the storage medium; backup is the Customer's responsibility (Section 10(2))
  • on-site work, remote access, training and consultancy

Chargeable additional services. The Provider offers migration of an existing Bambuddy installation (Docker or bare metal) to the Appliance at a fixed price. This covers transferring the existing data and printer connections into a new Appliance installation. It does not cover rebuilding the Customer's own containers, automations or other system modifications, nor restoring incomplete or corrupted data. It is a separate contract and not part of the subscription.

Case-by-case limits. Support is limited to the subject matter described in this annex and to an extent customary for a single installation. Requests beyond that may be offered by the Provider as separate consultancy.
