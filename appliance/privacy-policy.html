2. Hosting

This website is hosted by a third-party provider. When you visit this website, the hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files that your browser transmits. This includes:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL
  • Date and time of access

This data is processed based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, as it is necessary for the secure and efficient operation of this website. Server log files are deleted automatically after 30 days.

3. Analytics (Matomo)

We use Matomo, a self-hosted, open-source web analytics platform, to understand how visitors use this website. Matomo runs on our own infrastructure; no data is shared with third parties, and no data leaves the European Union.

Our Matomo installation is configured to process data anonymously:

  • No cookies are set in your browser
  • No personal identifiers (user ID, order ID, referrer paths beyond the domain) are stored
  • IP addresses are truncated by 2 bytes (e.g. 144.76.176.12 is stored as 144.76.0.0) before being written to the database, so no individual visitor can be identified
  • The anonymized IP is also used for geolocation (country-level only)
  • Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals are honored — if your browser sends DNT, no data is recorded
  • Raw visit logs are automatically deleted after 180 days

The following anonymous, aggregated data is collected:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referrer domain (how you arrived at the site)
  • Browser and device type
  • Approximate country (from the truncated IP)

Because the data is anonymized at the point of collection and no cookies or personal identifiers are used, this processing does not require consent under the GDPR. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest) — our legitimate interest being the operation and improvement of this website.

You have the right to object to this processing at any time under Art. 21 GDPR by enabling Do Not Track in your browser or by contacting us at martin@bambuddy.cool.

4. User Reviews

If you submit a review on our website, the following data is collected:

  • Your review text
  • Your rating (1-5 stars)
  • Your name (optional)

Reviews are stored using Supabase, a cloud database service. Supabase processes data in accordance with GDPR. Reviews are moderated before being displayed publicly. You can request deletion of your review by contacting us at martin@bambuddy.cool.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent through voluntary submission).

5. Bug Reports

If you submit a bug report through this website, the following data may be collected:

  • Bug description
  • Your email address (optional)
  • System information and logs (optional)
  • Screenshots (optional)

Bug reports are used to create GitHub issues for tracking and resolution. Personal information (IP addresses, serial numbers, names) is automatically sanitized from logs before submission. Your email address, if provided, is only used to follow up on the reported issue.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent through voluntary submission).

6. Contact by Email

If you contact us by email, your data (email address, name, message content) will be stored and processed for the purpose of handling your request. This data will not be shared with third parties without your consent.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to inquiries).

7. Business and Appliance Enquiry Forms

If you send an enquiry through the form on the Business or Appliance page, we receive your email address, your message and the details you select (topic, number of printers, region, timeframe, and whether you use Bambuddy for business). The form forwards them to us by email so we can reply; they are not stored on the website server.

To stop abuse, the form service keeps a short-lived per-IP-address counter in memory and records your IP address, but not your email address or message, in its server log. A successful enquiry is counted as an anonymous goal in our Matomo analytics (see section 3).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to a contract at your request) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protection against abuse).

9. Appliance Registration

A Bambuddy Appliance registers with our licence service and then reports roughly once a day. It sends only: a device identifier derived from the board's serial number, the batch identifier of the image, the licence key, the hardware model and the Appliance software version. Once an hour it also checks its licence status; that request carries only the device identifier and a device token, and we do not log it.

No print data, files, printer data, user data or credentials are ever transmitted. Your IP address is not stored in the clear: we keep only a salted, truncated hash of it, and only to detect one licence key being used on several devices.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (preventing misuse of licence keys). The registration is kept while the licence and the device registration exist; ask us at support@bambuddy.cool to delete it once you no longer use the Appliance.

10. Bambuddy Orders Licence Check

Bambuddy Orders runs on your own server. When you activate it, and then roughly once a day, it contacts our licence service. It sends only: a random installation identifier, the licence key (once, at activation; it is not stored in your installation), afterwards an installation token issued by the licence service, the Orders version and the domain of the connected shop. We use the shop domain to match support requests to your licence and to detect a licence being used for shops other than your own.

No orders, buyer data, print data or credentials for your shop or for Bambuddy are ever transmitted. Your shop credentials stay encrypted in your installation. Order data in your installation is processed by you, not by us. Bambuddy Orders stores neither the names nor the email or postal addresses of your buyers.

If you buy a Bambuddy Orders subscription, section 8 applies accordingly.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (preventing misuse of licence keys). The installation record is kept while the licence exists; ask us at support@bambuddy.cool to delete it after the licence has ended.
