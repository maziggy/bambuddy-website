First, the part most vendors leave out

Bambuddy is licensed under the AGPL-3.0. If you run it inside your own business — one printer or an entire floor of them — you owe us nothing. No seat count, no per-printer fee, no licence to buy. That is not a trial or a loophole; it is the licence, and it is not going to change.

So this page is not asking you to pay for permission. It is offering the things an open-source licence cannot give you: someone accountable when a print farm stops printing, a licence that covers redistribution if you need one, and time from the person who wrote the software.

Your files stay on your network

Bambuddy is self-hosted, and it runs perfectly well with no outbound connectivity at all. For design studios, labs and anyone under an NDA, that is usually the whole question — and it is answered by where the software runs, not by a policy document.

One caveat worth hearing before you ask, rather than after: whether a printer talks to Bambu Lab's cloud is a setting on the printer, not something Bambuddy controls. Bambuddy works fully in LAN-only mode, but putting your printers there is a change you make on the machines themselves.

Found a bug, or need a feature? That goes on GitHub, not here. File an issue — it is public, other people running your fleet size find it and pile on, and that is what gets something prioritised and fixed for everyone. It is free, and it is faster than we are. We do not provide email support for the open-source software, and a support contract does not change that — it changes where your issue sits in the queue.

Bambuddy is free and open source, and so is help with running it — that lives on GitHub, where the answer is public and everyone running your setup benefits from it. Installation and configuration are covered in the documentation. Send this anyway if you would like us to see it — it shows us what the docs are missing — but expect the answer on GitHub, not by return email.
